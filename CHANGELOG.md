# Classic or Not

## 0.1.0

First release. A small blue infinity marks what is not from the original game:

- **Quests**: at quest givers (gossip list, greeting list, quest text before you accept) and in the quest log; on EllesmereUI's quest tracker too when it is installed.
- **Recipes**: in the profession window and at profession trainers.
- **Items**: a "New in Forever" line on the tooltip of every new item (gear, recipes, mount and pet items, toys).
- **Collections**: in the mount journal, the pet journal, the toy box and the appearances.
- Every place can be turned off (`/classicornot`); the options are in the game's options, and in the EllesmereUI panel too if you tick the box.
