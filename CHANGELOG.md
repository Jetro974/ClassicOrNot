# Classic or Not

## Unreleased

- **Library books**: a new tab in the collections journal, laid out like the toy box, shows Forever's library books: to hand in (in your bags or bank), to find, and handed in, with a counter, the reward steps, a search box, a Filter menu and where each book is. Click a book to pin its spot on the map.
- **Merchants**: the mark on new items in a merchant's list and buyback list.
- **Library books, tooltip and chat**: a book's tooltip says whether you still have to hand it in; a chat line when you loot one.
- **Library books on the maps** (off by default): the books you still have to find on the world map and the minimap.

## 0.1.0

First release. A small blue infinity marks what is not from the original game:

- **Quests**: at quest givers (gossip list, greeting list, quest text before you accept) and in the quest log.
- **Recipes**: in the profession window and at profession trainers.
- **Items**: a "New in Forever" line on the tooltip of every new item (gear, recipes, mount and pet items, toys).
- **Collections**: in the mount journal, the pet journal, the toy box and the appearances; mounts, pets and appearances can also be filtered (All, New in Forever, Classic) from the game's Filter menu; one box shows the mounts and pets you don't have yet, with the journals' Filter button that Forever hides.
- Every place can be turned off (`/classicornot`); the options are in the game's options (Options > AddOns).
