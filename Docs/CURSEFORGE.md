## Is it Classic... or not?

WoW Forever mixes the original game with a lot of new content. **Classic or Not** tells you at a glance what is new: a small blue infinity marks everything that was not in the original game.

### Where you see it

- **Quests**: in a quest giver's list, on the quest text before you accept it, and in your quest log.
- **Recipes**: in your profession window and at profession trainers.
- **Items**: a *New in Forever* line on the tooltip of every new item, in your bags, at vendors, in chat links. Gear, recipes, mount and pet items, toys: all of them.
- **Collections**: new mounts in the mount journal, new pets in the pet journal, new toys in the toy box.

Every place can be turned off on its own. Type `/classicornot` or use the addon button on the minimap.

### Works with EllesmereUI

With EllesmereUI installed, Classic or Not settles into the EllesmereUI panel as its own section, and marks new quests on EllesmereUI's quest tracker too.

### Accurate, and nothing to update

"Original game" is read from the game's own data, on the last Classic Era client before Season of Discovery. No hand-made lists, no guesses: and since the original game never changes, anything Forever adds later is marked automatically.

### Light

Nothing runs for a place you turn off, nothing polls, and Blizzard's frames are never modified: the marks are drawn on frames of the addon's own.
