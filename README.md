# Classic or Not – What's New in Forever

A World of Warcraft: Forever addon that marks, with a small blue infinity, what is not from the original game:

- **Quests**: at quest givers and in the quest log.
- **Recipes**: in the profession window and at profession trainers.
- **Items**: a "New in Forever" line on the tooltip of any new item (gear, recipes, mount and pet items, toys), and the mark on new items at merchants.
- **Collections**: in the mount journal, the pet journal, the toy box and the appearances. Mounts, pets and appearances can also be filtered: All, New in Forever or Classic, in the game's own Filter menu. Forever hides the mount and pet journals' Filter button: one box brings it back, with the mounts and pets you don't have yet.

Every place can be turned off in the options (`/classicornot`). The options are in the game's options (Options > AddOns).

"Original game" means the game's own data on the last Classic Era client before Season of Discovery, so nothing needs updating when Forever adds content: anything new is marked.
